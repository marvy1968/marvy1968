### 2. Book Methods: Wrong Translations for FX

FX is a 24/5 OTC market with no central volume, no daily "gap," and broker-specific ticks. Here is how your standard adaptations are likely failing, and the correct translation in one line:

1.  **Opening Range Breakout (M15).** 
    *   *The mistake:* Treating a time of day (like 8:00 AM NY) as an "open" and looking for gaps/volume rushes. FX doesn't open; it transitions.
    *   *Correct translation:* **Asian Range Breakout:** Define the range as highest-high/lowest-low between 00:00 and 07:00 London time; place stop-buy/stop-sell orders at the boundaries at 07:00, cancel at 11:00.
2.  **Floor-Pivot S1/R1 Bounce (M15/H1).**
    *   *The mistake:* Using midnight local time or GMT to define the Daily High/Low/Close. Bank rollover (and institutional pivot calculation) happens at the New York close.
    *   *Correct translation:* Calculate all daily pivots strictly using the **17:00 ET (New York) close**, and limit bounces to overlapping sessions (e.g., London/NY overlap 8:00-11:00 ET).
3.  **Raschke-Connors Turtle Soup (False Breakout).** 
    *   *The mistake:* Waiting for a candle close-confirmation. If a 20-period low is broken on an H1/H4 chart and you wait for the bar to close back inside the range, the retail spread and the length of the candle will eat your entire R-multiple.
    *   *Correct translation:* **Blind Limit Entry:** Place a limit order 5 pips *inside* the 20-period extreme, triggered immediately when price sweeps the extreme and reverts, with a hard stop 5 pips below the sweep low.
4.  **Carter TTM Squeeze.**
    *   *The mistake:* Using broker tick-volume as a proxy for the momentum trigger. Tick volume in FX is just quote frequency and varies wildly by broker.
    *   *Correct translation:* Remove volume entirely; trigger purely on ATR expansion (current ATR > 1.5x 20-period ATR) aligning with the slope of a 50 EMA.
