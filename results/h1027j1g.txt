But in retail spot FX, the broker (OANDA) sets the overnight financing (rollover) rates. They do not give you the interbank rate. They apply an asymmetric markup. * **Data Sources (Execution):** OANDA Daily (D1) bid/ask candles at New York close (17:00 ET).
* **The Exact Rule:** 
  1. Calculate the daily Yield Differential: `Rate(Base) - Rate(Quote)`. (e.g., for AUD/USD, Rate_AUD - Rate_USD).
  2. **Long Rule:** If Yield Diff > 0 AND Price > 200-day SMA, go Long.
  3. **Short Rule:** If Yield Diff < 0 AND Price < 200-day SMA, go Short.
  4. Flatten if the Price crosses back over the 200-day SMA (trend is broken). Rebalance monthly or upon a cross of the SMA.
* **Sizing Across 7 Pairs:** Inverse volatility (ATR parity). 1 Daily ATR = 0.5% of account equity. Do not use fixed lot sizes, because GBP/JPY and EUR/CHF have drastically different volatility profiles. Cap total account exposure at 3 open positions at any time (rank by highest absolute yield differential).
* **Avoiding Carry Crashes (2008, 2015, 2022):**

  2. Add a macro circuit breaker: **VIX > 30 = flatten all FX carry positions immediately.** (Carry trades are essentially shorting global volatility; when the VIX spikes, funding currencies like USD and JPY surge).

  * **Train:** 2005 to 2015 (captures the 2008 GFC carry crash and early ZIRP).

* **The Control:** A pure 200-day Trend Filter with no interest rate logic (e.g., go long if Price > 200 SMA, short if Price < 200 SMA, equal weight across all 7 pairs).
