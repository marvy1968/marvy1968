## 1. Pipeline (all indicators on CLOSED bars; signal at close t, act at open t+1)
Base TF = Daily (entries/exits). HTF = Weekly (last CLOSED week only). Intraday VWAP is unavailable on daily bars → **VWAP = anchored VWAP from the most recent confirmed swing low (long) / swing high (short)**, swing = 5-bar fractal (confirmed 2 bars after pivot, no lookahead). Robustness alt: weekly VWAP (reset each Monday) — report both, pick on train only.

### 1.1 Stage filter (Weinstein, weekly)
- MA30w = 30-week SMA of weekly close; slope = MA30w_t − MA30w_{t−4}.
- STAGE 2 (long-eligible): weekly close > MA30w AND slope > 0. STAGE 4 (short-eligible): close < MA30w AND slope < 0. Else ineligible.

### 1.2 Sector filter (proxy for "sector news")
- 11 SPDR sectors: XLK XLF XLV XLY XLP XLE XLI XLB XLU XLRE(from 2015-10) XLC(from 2018-06; before that map to XLK/XLY per GICS-then).
- RS_63 = ETF 63-day return − SPY 63-day return, computed at prior close. Rank weekly (Friday close, used next week).
- LONG sectors = top 3 ranks; SHORT sectors = bottom 3. Sweep (train only): lookback {42,63,126}, top/bottom N {2,3}.

### 1.3 Stock selection (top 3 per eligible sector)
Candidates: S&P 500 members of that sector, passing stage filter for the side, price > $10, 20d avg $vol > $20M.
Score = strength_daily (pacer 1.1: N=20 slope/ATR14 · sqrt(R2)) with confirmation gates:
- HTF: weekly strength same sign, |strength_w| >= 0.05.
- Volume: 20d avg volume / 100d avg volume >= 0.9 (participation not drying up).
- Volatility: ATR14/close between 1% and 6%.
- Candlestick (reporting-only feature, NOT a gate in A): last bar close in upper 1/3 of range (long).
Pick top 3 by |score| per sector → watchlist (≤9 long, ≤9 short), refreshed weekly.

### 1.4 Entry — Variant A (main): 2 non-redundant triggers
LONG on watchlist name when BOTH at close t:
1. **Buy line (pullback):** low_t <= SMA20_t + 0.25·ATR AND close_t > SMA20_t − 0.5·ATR (touched/near 20MA, did not break).
2. **Timing:** Stoch(14,3,3) %K crossed above %D within last 3 bars with %D_min(last 5) < 30 (rising from low).
Enter at open t+1 (market). RSI, Bollinger, VWAP, S/R are NOT entry gates in A. Short = mirror (high near SMA20, %K crosses below %D from >70).
Variant A2 (ablation, report only): add "close > anchored VWAP" + "within S/R buy zone + RSI14 40–55" to show whether extra filters add edge or just cut trades.

### 1.5 Initial stop & R
- S/R zone: nearest support = lowest low of last confirmed swing low within 40 bars (zone = [swing low − 0.25 ATR, swing low]).
- stop_long = min( zone bottom , SMA20 − 1.0·ATR ) — use the *nearer* of the two if it is >= 1.0 ATR from entry, else the farther. Hard floor: stop distance >= 1.0 ATR, cap <= 3.0 ATR (skip trade if > 3 ATR).
- **R = entry − stop** (short: stop − entry).

### 1.6 Exits — Variant A (main)
- TP1: sell 1/3 at entry + 1R. On TP1 fill → stop on remainder to breakeven (entry + round-trip cost).
- TP2: sell 1/3 at entry + 1.5R.
- Runner 1/3 — **stair-step trail** (updated at each close, only ratchets up): trail = max(current trail, latest confirmed higher swing low − 0.1·ATR, SMA20 − 0.25·ATR).
- Trend-break exit (all remaining): 3 consecutive closes below BOTH anchored VWAP and SMA20 → exit at next open.
- TP signal (A, runner only, optional tighten): if RSI14 >= 70 OR close >= SMA20·1.08 OR close >= BB(20,2) upper → tighten trail to that bar's low. No time stop except 60 bars max hold.

### 1.7 Exits — Variant B (Marvin original)
- Scale out 1/4 each at 0.5R, 0.75R, 1R, 1.5R. Stair-step trail: after 0.5R move stop to −0.5R, after 1R to breakeven, then trail under each higher swing low.
- Full exit on TP signal: Stoch %K >= 70 or RSI >= 70, or close >= 8% above SMA20, or close >= upper BB.
- Full SELL on: stochastic turning down from >70 (%K cross below %D) OR 3 closes below VWAP and SMA20.
- Same entries (1.4 A) so exits are the only difference. Also run B-entries (all of: stoch rising, close > VWAP, near 20MA) × B-exits as "B-full".

### 1.8 Portfolio
- One position per symbol; max 8 concurrent (≤5 per side, ≤2 per sector). Risk 0.75% equity per trade (sweep {0.5,1.0}% reporting only); shares = floor(risk$/R). Gross exposure cap 150%.
- Signal ties → highest score first.

### Pseudo-code
```
for day t (time order, all symbols merged):
    process open positions on bar t: stop/TP/trail (3.3 rules)
    at close t: if Friday -> recompute stage(weekly closed), sector RS rank, watchlist

        update trails/trend-break counters with close t
```

## 3. Testing instructions (for Claude) — identical protocol to pacer spec §3
3.1 Strict time order single event loop; signal at close t, earliest fill open t+1. Unit-test: shifting future data must not change past signals (incl. swing pivots, anchored VWAP, weekly stage).

## 4. Pass bar (per variant A / B, long-only and long+short)
ALL required: holdout PF > 1.1 after costs; >= 100 holdout trades; train PF > 1.0 with positive return; beats random >=16/20 AND shuffled >=16/20; PF > 1.0 at 2× costs. Fail any → FAIL. PF computed on per-trade R (all partials of one trade combined).

- Many filters → few trades: if holdout < 100 trades, relax only via train-chosen sweep, never on holdout.
- Variant B's 0.5R/0.75R partials are cost-sensitive (24 bps vs small R) — report avg R in bps vs cost.
- Benchmark to beat: Sector Top-Down PF 1.54/11y. PF > 2 or smooth equity = assume bug.
