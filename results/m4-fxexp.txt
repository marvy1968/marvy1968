# Forex notes that matter for these systems

General market facts come from standard references: the BIS Triennial Survey 2022, OANDA and CME education pages, and Kathy Lien's book (chapters on sessions and pairs). Numbers marked **measured** I worked out myself fr

## Sessions (New York time; CT is one hour earlier)
| Session | NY time (approx.) | Character |
|---|---|---|
| Sydney/Tokyo (Asia) | 17:00/19:00 – 03:00/04:00 | Quietest for EUR, GBP, CHF and CAD. JPY, AUD and NZD are active. Ranges are often *set* here and then tested in London. |
| London | 03:00 – 12:00 | Largest share of turnover (BIS 2022: UK about 38%). Most daily highs and lows are made here. Breakouts of the Asian range start here. |
| New York | 08:00 – 17:00 | US data comes out at 08:30 NY. London/NY overlap 08:00–12:00 is the most liquid window. Liquidity dries up after the London close (about 11:00–12:00). |
| Rollover | 16:55 – 17:15 | Daily candle boundary, financing booked, **spreads widen several-fold**. Weekend: market closes Fri 17:00 and reopens Sun about 17:00. |

**Measured:** GBP/USD average hourly range (2024-26, Dukascopy H1, by NY hour):
- 9-11 pips from 16:00 to 01:00
- 17-21 pips in early London (02:00-07:00)
- 24-28 pips at 08:00-10:00 (the overlap)
- Tapering to about 12 pips by 15:00

How this shapes the systems:
- Every entry is a resting **stop order at OANDA**, so fills happen whenever the price gets there, in any session.
- The runner never places orders during the rollover. It waits until 17:15 NY.
- Crabel's "open" is the 17:00 NY daily open. That is a thin, wide-spread moment, so it is a known weak point of moving an equity-futures ORB to FX.

## Spreads and costs
Typical OANDA US spreads in liquid hours (what the backtest uses, deliberately a bit wide):

| Pair | Spread (pips) |
|---|---|
| EUR/USD | 1.4 |
| USD/JPY | 1.4 |
| AUD/USD | 1.4 |
| USD/CHF | 1.8 |
| GBP/USD | 2.0 |
| NZD/USD | 2.0 |
| EUR/JPY | 2.0 |
| USD/CAD | 2.2 |
| GBP/JPY | 3.0 |

- Spreads are 2-10× wider at rollover, at the Sunday open, and in the seconds around NFP, CPI, FOMC and central-bank decisions.
- Spread as a fraction of the stop is what matters. A 30-pip stop on GBP/JPY pays about 10% of its risk in spread alone. A 150-pip Turtle stop pays about 1-2%.

## Pips and pip values
- A pip is 0.0001, or 0.01 for JPY pairs. OANDA quotes to 1/10 pip.
- For a USD account, 10,000 units of a XXX/USD pair = $1 per pip.
- For USD/XXX and crosses the dollar value moves with the rate: pip $ = units × pip ÷ (USD/quote rate).
- The sizing code (`fxexp/sizing.py`) converts every pair to USD risk.

Considered and not chosen:
- **Larry Connors RSI(2)**: it is a stock/ETF strategy, and Connors argues *against* stops, which conflicts with "book stops at the broker".

## Common rules (all five systems)
- **Data and clock:** OANDA daily candles aligned to 17:00 New York. Signals come only from *completed* daily candles. Orders for "today" are placed after 17:15 NY, once the rollover spread has settled.
- **Stops and targets live at OANDA:**
  - Stop-entry orders carry `stopLossOnFill` and, where the book has a target, `takeProfitOnFill`.
  - Trailing exits are done by moving the broker stop once a day.
  - Day trades are closed at 16:50 NY.
- **Sizing (DEFAULT):** 0.5% of NAV at risk per trade, per system. Configurable from 0.5% to 1% (`risk_pct`). Margin cap: at most 25% of NAV in margin per position.
- **One trade per pair per system.** When one side of an OCO fills, the other side is cancelled.
- **Spread-aware:** no order is placed or sent while the live spread is more than 2.5× the pair's typical spread. Stop entries carry `priceBound` = level ± 3× typical spread, so a gap through the level cancels the ord
- **Pairs:** EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, AUD/USD, USD/CAD, USD/CHF, NZD/USD, EUR/JPY, GBP/JPY. The same universe for every system; not chosen by backtest.
- **No news filter.** We have no calendar feed; see FOREX_NOTES.
- **US account rules:** FIFO and no hedging. The recommended setup is **one OANDA practice sub-account per system**.

## 1. Kathy Lien – The Fader (`lien-fader`)
**BOOK** (strategy rules, reprinted verbatim at TradeWins Daily, "The Forex Fader Strategy"; exa.ai abstract of Lien's chapter, 2015):

Long:
1. "Locate a currency pair whose 14-period ADX is less than 35. Ideally the ADX should also be trending downward."
2. "Wait for the market to break below the previous day's low by at least 15 pips."
3. "Place an entry order to buy 15 pips above the previous day's high."
4. "After getting filled, place your initial stop no more than 30 pips away."
5. "Take profit on the position when prices increase by double your risk, or 60 pips."

Short is the mirror image. The chapter adds: "It uses the daily charts to identify the range-bound environment and the hourly charts to pinpoint entry levels." It also says it works best without major data releases.

**Ours:**
- **Filter:** daily ADX(14) < 35. The "ideally falling" part is optional and not coded.
- **Arm:** price trades 15 pips beyond the prior day's low (or high).
- **Entry:** stop order 15 pips beyond the opposite prior-day extreme.
- **Stop and target:** stop exactly 30 pips from the fill (`stopLossOnFill.distance`); target 60 pips (2R).
- **Timeframe and validity:** daily setup, intraday stop entry, order good for the day (GTD 17:00 NY, DEFAULT). The trade is held until stop or target; the book has no time exit.

## 2. Raschke & Connors – Turtle Soup (`raschke-soup`)
**BOOK** (Street Smarts ch. 4 rules as reprinted by TradingMarkets/Connors Group, 2003):
1. "The futures contract must make a new 20-day low."
2. "The previous 20-day low must have occurred at least four trading sessions earlier."
3. "After the market falls below the previous 20-day low, place an entry buy stop 5-10 ticks above the prior 20-day low. This buy stop will be good for today only."
4. "If the trade is triggered, place an initial good-until-cancelled sell stop one tick under today's low."

Also from the book:
- The chapter text: trail the stop as the trade becomes profitable. If stopped out on day 1 or 2, re-enter at the original entry level.
- The Turtle Soup Plus One text: "take profits within two to six days and trail stops."

# OANDA v20 REST API — notes for fx-experts

Sources: developer.oanda.com/rest-live-v20 (Introduction, Development Guide, Best Practices, Order/Trade/Instrument/Pricing definitions). Read on 2026-10-02.

## Environments and auth
| | Practice (fxTrade Practice) | Live (fxTrade) |
|---|---|---|
| REST | `https://api-fxpractice.oanda.com` | `https://api-fxtrade.oanda.com` |
| Streaming | `https://stream-fxpractice.oanda.com` | `https://stream-fxtrade.oanda.com` |

- Every request needs the header `Authorization: Bearer <personal access token>`. Practice and live tokens are separate. A token is made (or revoked and re-made) under "Manage API Access" in the OANDA hub. Making a new t
- Account IDs look like `101-001-XXXXXXXX-001`. `101` = practice (live starts `001`). The second group is the division: `001` = OANDA Corporation (US, NFA rules).
- `GET /v3/accounts` lists the accounts a token can use. Sub-accounts (`…-002`, `…-003`) are created in the hub, not through the API.
- Send `Accept-Datetime-Format: RFC3339` to get ISO times (the other option is UNIX).

## Rate limits and connection practice (Best Practices page)
- At most **2 new connections per second**.
- At most **100 requests per second** on a persistent (keep-alive) connection.
- Use one `requests.Session`. Our client spaces calls ≥60 ms apart and backs off on 429/5xx.
- For a live account view: `GET /accounts/{id}` once, then `GET /accounts/{id}/changes?sinceTransactionID=…`. Our 5-minute one-shot runner only needs `openTrades`, `pendingOrders`, `summary` and `pricing`.

## Market data
- `GET /v3/instruments/{instrument}/candles` takes these parameters:
  - `granularity`: S5…M1, M5, M15, H1, H4, D, W, M
  - `price`: M (mid), B (bid), A (ask), or a combination like `MBA`
  - `count` (max **5000**), or `from` / `to`
  - `dailyAlignment` (default 17) and `alignmentTimezone` (default America/New_York): daily candles roll at **17:00 New York**
  - `weeklyAlignment`
  - `smooth`
- Each candle has `complete: true/false`. Only complete candles are used for signals.
- `GET /v3/accounts/{id}/pricing?instruments=A,B` returns current bids/asks, `tradeable`, and the `closeoutBid/Ask` used for margin. Spread = ask − bid.
- `GET /v3/accounts/{id}/instruments` returns:
  - `pipLocation` (−4 for EUR_USD, −2 for JPY pairs)
  - `displayPrecision` (5 or 3)
  - `tradeUnitsPrecision` (0 = whole units)
  - `minimumTradeSize`
  - `marginRate`
  - `minimumTrailingStopDistance`
  - `guaranteedStopLossOrderMode`, etc.

## Orders (POST /v3/accounts/{id}/orders, body `{"order": {...}}`)
- **MARKET**: `timeInForce` must be FOK (default) or IOC. Optional `priceBound` = worst acceptable price.
- **LIMIT / STOP / MARKET_IF_TOUCHED**: price threshold; `timeInForce` GTC (default), GTD + `gtdTime`, or **GFD** (cancelled at 17:00 NY). A STOP fills at "equal or worse" than its price. If the market gaps through both 
- `triggerCondition`: DEFAULT (ask for buys, bid for sells), INVERSE, BID, ASK, MID. Orders always *fill* on their default side.
- `positionFill`: DEFAULT, OPEN_ONLY, REDUCE_FIRST, REDUCE_ONLY. We use **OPEN_ONLY** so one system can never reduce another system's position.
- Dependent orders created when the entry fills:
  - `takeProfitOnFill {price}`
  - `stopLossOnFill {price | distance}`: `distance` is measured from the fill, so a book's "30 pips from entry" is exact even after slippage
  - `trailingStopLossOnFill {distance}`
  - `guaranteedStopLossOnFill`: needs GSLO enabled on the account, costs a premium, and has a minimum distance
- `clientExtensions {id, tag, comment}` labels the order. `tradeClientExtensions` labels the trade it opens. We tag every order and trade `tag=fxexp`, `comment=<system id>`, so the runner and the scorecard only ever touc
- Change a pending order: `PUT /orders/{id}` (cancel/replace: you get a new order ID, so use the client ID). Cancel: `PUT /orders/{id}/cancel`.
- Trade management:
  - `PUT /trades/{id}/orders` with `{"stopLoss": {...}, "takeProfit": {...}, "trailingStopLoss": {...}}` creates, replaces or cancels the trade's dependent orders. This is how we trail stops.
  - `PUT /trades/{id}/close` with `{"units":"ALL"}` closes the trade.
  - `GET /trades?state=CLOSED` lists closed trades.
  - Positions: `GET /positions`, `PUT /positions/{instr}/close`.

- Units = base-currency amount. +units = long, −units = short. Minimum 1 unit.
- Pip = 10^pipLocation: 0.0001, or 0.01 for JPY pairs. Prices quote to 1/10 pip.
- P&L per unit is in the quote currency, converted to the account currency (USD). Sizing formula: `units = NAV × risk% / (|entry − stop| × quote→USD)`.
  - quote→USD is 1 for XXX_USD.

# fx-experts: VM deployment plan (prepared, NOT installed)

Target: instance-stockbot, `/opt/fx-experts`, user moonrider1968.

Nothing existing is touched:
- no existing unit, cron entry, env file or directory is modified, restarted or stopped;
- the new units are named `fx-experts*`.

## Before installing (needs Marvin)
1. **Make a new OANDA *practice* API token** (OANDA hub → Manage API Access). Every OANDA token on the VM currently returns 401.
   - Regenerating the token invalidates the old one. The old ones already fail, so this changes nothing for the other bots unless Marvin also puts the new token into their env files. That is his call.
2. Recommended: in the OANDA hub, **create 5 practice sub-accounts**, one per system. The account is US (FIFO, no hedging), so systems sharing a pair in one account would otherwise block each other.
3. Approve the deploy.

## Install steps (run by the agent after approval)
1. Copy `fx-experts-bundle.tgz` to `/tmp` on the VM and untar it to `/tmp/fx-experts`.
2. Run `bash /tmp/fx-experts/deploy/install.sh`. It:
   - creates `/opt/fx-experts` and a venv;
   - creates `.env` (mode 600) from env.example;
   - runs the unit tests;
   - runs the read-only preflight (`python -m fxexp.check`): token, practice account IDs and spreads, with no orders;
   - does one `--dry-run` pass (computes orders, sends nothing);
   - installs the four unit files, **not enabled**.
3. Fill in `/opt/fx-experts/.env`:
   - the token;
   - the account ID or IDs;
   - TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN / TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID, copied from the existing scorecard env.
4. Enable: `sudo systemctl enable --now fx-experts.timer fx-experts-scorecard.timer`.

## What runs
| Unit | When | What |
|---|---|---|
| `fx-experts.timer` → `fx-experts.service` | every 5 min (no-op Fri 17:00 – Sun 17:00 NY) | one-shot runner: daily plan after 17:15 NY, arming/placing broker-side stop entries with SL/TP, daily stop trailing, Crabel
| `fx-experts-scorecard.timer` | Mon-Fri 16:20 CT | send-only Telegram scorecard per system (day / week / total P&L, trades, win %, open) |

- **Logs:** `/opt/fx-experts/logs/runner.log` and `/opt/scorecards/logs/fx-experts.log`.
- **State:** `/opt/fx-experts/state/live_state.json`. If it is lost, broker-side stops still protect every trade.

## Safety
- `OANDA_ENV` must be `practice`. The client also refuses the live host unless `FX_ALLOW_LIVE=YES`, which is never set.
- Secrets are only in the mode-600 `.env`. The unit files contain no secrets.
- **Kill switch:** set `"no_new_entries": true` in config.json (existing trades keep their broker stops), or `sudo systemctl disable --now fx-experts.timer`.
