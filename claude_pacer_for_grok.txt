# In-game basketball prop pacer: design + walk-forward backtest (for Grok)

Nov 7 2026. NBA, NCAA men's, NCAA women's. All results below come from a season that no model saw in training
(train 2023-25, test 2025-26), and for the NBA from real Kalshi prices at the quarter breaks.

## TL;DR

1. **A straight-line pacer loses money and should be retired.** "Stat so far × game length ÷ minutes played" is the
   worst model in every league, and against Kalshi prices it lost **−8.2%** after fees on 1,176 bets. Its
   90% range was −14% to −3%, so this is not bad luck. When it says 73% the outcome happens 53% of the time: it
   overrates hot starts.
2. **The fix is to project minutes, not the rate.** A hot or cold first half barely predicts the rest of the game.
   The fitted weight on tonight's per-minute rate after 24 minutes on the floor is only about 15% for points (NBA).
   What actually moves a player's final line is **remaining minutes**: blowouts, foul trouble, whether he is on the
   floor, and his normal rotation share.
3. **The minutes model** (8 fitted numbers) and a gradient-boosted version of it are the best in all three leagues,
   with near-perfect calibration on the unseen season.
4. **Against Kalshi (NBA, 68 games, Jan-May 2026):** the minutes model made **+8.8%** after fees on 469 bets at
   an edge of 6c or more. The 90% range was −1.8% to +17.8% and the chance of truly positive ROI is 92%. **That is a
   lead, not proof**: on the earlier half of that sample, used to choose settings, it was about break-even.
   The profit is in **NO / Under bets (+13%)**; YES bets lost (−6%).
5. Most Kalshi in-game prop quotes are untradeable: only 22% had a bid/ask gap of 10c or less at the breaks.

## The minutes model (`models.py: v2_minutes, v2_mean`; called "v2" in code)

At a break, for one player and one stat:

```
share       = (1 - w) * pregame_minutes/game_length + w * minutes_so_far/elapsed,   w = elapsed / (elapsed + tau)
rem_minutes = share * minutes_left * foul_factor * blowout_factor * (bench if not on the floor at the break)
rate        = (1 - v) * pregame_per_minute + v * tonight_per_minute,                v = minutes_played / (minutes_played + kappa[stat])
rem_mean    = rate * rem_minutes * (tonight's team pace / usual pace) ^ pace_beta
P(final >= line) = negative binomial with variance = phi(mean) * mean, phi fitted on train residuals
```

`foul_factor = exp(-foul * fouls above (foul_out - 1) * fraction_played + 0.5)`, and 0 when fouled out.
`blowout_factor = exp(-blow * max(|margin| - 12, 0)/10 * fraction_played/0.75 * pregame_share)`.
Pregame anchors are each player's exponentially weighted averages (half-life 10 games) from **earlier games only**.

Fitted parameters (Poisson deviance, train seasons):

| | tau | kappa pts | kappa reb | kappa ast | kappa 3PM | foul | blow | pace_beta |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NBA | 32.8 | 144 | 186 | 41 | 244 | 0.13 | 0.42 | 0.17 |
| NCAA men's | 34.1 | 93 | 97 | 142 | 168 | 0.18 | 0.00 | 0.32 |
| NCAA women's | 36.9 | 93 | 101 | 145 | 165 | 0.31 | 0.08 | 0.31 |

A large kappa means "trust the pregame rate". For points, rebounds and threes the model gives tonight's rate little weight.
Assists (NBA) are the exception: they follow tonight's role faster.

Suggested change: replace these constants with the measured ones, per league. College blowouts barely cost minutes.

## Recommendation

- Use the minutes model (or ML) to produce `p_model_raw` for `pacer_v2/`, instead of the straight-line pacer.
- Paper only, NO side first, edge of 6c or more after fee, bid/ask gap of 10c or less, and only quotes refreshed after the period ended.
- Promote only after 100+ graded live paper bets beat break-even with positive closing value.
  The +8.8% has a 90% range that includes zero.
- Before using it on Bovada: Bovada's live lines are a different market. Run the same test on Bovada lines from
  `state/live_props/shadow.jsonl` once enough are logged.

## Reproduce

```bash
# data (public): sportsdataverse ESPN play-by-play
#   espn_nba_pbp/play_by_play_{2024,2025,2026}.parquet
#   espn_mens_college_basketball_pbp / espn_womens_college_basketball_pbp play_by_play_{2025,2026}.parquet
python research/prop_pacer/build.py nba_snap.parquet nba PBP_2024 PBP_2025 PBP_2026       # ~7 min
python research/prop_pacer/fetch_kalshi.py nba_snap_2026.parquet kalshi_breaks.jsonl 120 7  # ~2.5 h, ~3 req/s
PACER_SAVE_MARKET=market_rows.parquet python research/prop_pacer/evaluate.py nba_snap.parquet kalshi_breaks.jsonl out/
python research/prop_pacer/bootstrap.py market_rows.parquet
```

Checks done: rebuilt points equal the final score in every sampled game, and minutes sum to 240 per NBA team and 200 per
college team (OT included). Kalshi settlement agrees with the play-by-play box score on 100% of matched contracts.

Limits: one test season; Kalshi covers 68 games after quote filters; quotes are 1-minute candle closes, not a
fill simulation (no queue, size or slippage); college has no exchange props, so college results are accuracy only.

=================== research/prop_pacer/models.py ===================
"""In-game NBA prop models. Each predicts the MEAN of a player's remaining stat at a break; a shared, fitted
overdispersed count distribution turns the mean into P(final >= threshold).

  naive    straight-line pacer: so_far x 48 / minutes of game played
  prop1    Claude Prop 1 (marv/live_prop_engine.project): per-possession production x pace x script
  v2       minutes-first structural model (fitted on train seasons):
             remaining minutes = floor-time share x minutes left x foul / blowout / bench adjustments
                 share = blend(pregame minutes share, tonight's share), weight = elapsed / (elapsed + tau)
             per-minute rate  = blend(pregame per-minute rate, tonight's rate), weight = min_sf / (min_sf + kappa[stat])
             remaining mean   = rate x remaining minutes x (tonight's team pace / usual pace) ^ pace_beta
  ml       gradient-boosted Poisson regression of the remaining stat on the same inputs
"""

from __future__ import annotations

import numpy as np
import pandas as pd
from scipy import optimize, stats as sst

STATS = ["pts", "reb", "ast", "fg3"]
LEAGUE_DEFAULTS = {"nba": (48.0, 6), "ncaab": (40.0, 5), "ncaaw": (40.0, 5)}  # regulation minutes, foul-out count


def usable(df: pd.DataFrame) -> pd.DataFrame:
    """Rows with a pregame anchor (5+ earlier games) for players who have played tonight."""
    d = df[(df["pre_n"] >= 5) & (df["pre_min"] >= 8) & (df["min_sf"] > 0)].copy()
    if "league" not in d:
        d["league"] = "nba"
    for col, i in (("game_min", 0), ("foul_out", 1)):
        if col not in d:
            d[col] = d["league"].map(lambda lg: LEAGUE_DEFAULTS[lg][i])
    if "frac" not in d:
        d["frac"] = d["elapsed"] / d["game_min"]
    for s in STATS:
        d[f"{s}_rem"] = d[f"{s}_final"] - d[f"{s}_sf"]
    d["left"] = d["game_min"] - d["elapsed"]
    d["pace_ratio"] = ((d["tposs_sf"] / d["elapsed"] * 36) / d["pre_pace36"]).clip(0.75, 1.3).fillna(1.0)
    return d


# ---------------------------------------------------------------- naive + prop1


def naive(d: pd.DataFrame, s: str) -> np.ndarray:
    return (d[f"{s}_sf"] * d["game_min"] / d["elapsed"] - d[f"{s}_sf"]).to_numpy()


def prop1(d: pd.DataFrame, s: str) -> np.ndarray:
    import sys
    from pathlib import Path
    sys.path.insert(0, str(Path(__file__).resolve().parents[2]))
    from marv import live_prop_engine as E
    market = {"pts": "player_points", "reb": "player_rebounds", "ast": "player_assists", "fg3": "player_threes"}[s]
    out = []
    for r in d[[f"{s}_sf", f"pre_{s}", "elapsed", "tposs_sf", "margin", "pf_sf", "min_sf", "league"]].itertuples(index=False):
        st = E.PropState(r[7], market, "", float(r[0]), float(r[1]), float(r[2]), float(r[3]), float(r[4]),
                         fouls=float(r[5]), minutes=float(r[6]))
        out.append(E.project(st)["rem_mean"])
    return np.asarray(out)


# ---------------------------------------------------------------- v2 structural


def v2_minutes(d: pd.DataFrame, p: dict) -> np.ndarray:
    pre_share = (d["pre_min"] / d["game_min"]).clip(0, 0.95)
    live_share = (d["min_sf"] / d["elapsed"]).clip(0, 1)
    w = d["elapsed"] / (d["elapsed"] + p["tau"])
    share = (1 - w) * pre_share + w * live_share
    # foul trouble: fouls above the "safe" count for this point of the game cost minutes
    safe = (d["foul_out"] - 1) * d["frac"] + 0.5
    excess = (d["pf_sf"] - safe).clip(lower=0)
    foul = np.exp(-p["foul"] * excess) * np.where(d["pf_sf"] >= d["foul_out"], 0, 1)
    # blowout: starters sit when the margin is large late
    late = d["frac"] / 0.75
    blow = np.exp(-p["blow"] * ((d["margin"].abs() - 12).clip(lower=0) / 10) * late * pre_share)
    bench = np.where(d["on_court"], 1.0, p["bench"])
    return (share * d["left"] * foul * blow * bench).to_numpy()


def v2_mean(d: pd.DataFrame, s: str, p: dict) -> np.ndarray:
    pre_rate = (d[f"pre_{s}"] / d["pre_min"]).clip(lower=0)
    live_rate = (d[f"{s}_sf"] / d["min_sf"].clip(lower=1)).clip(lower=0)
    w = d["min_sf"] / (d["min_sf"] + p[f"kappa_{s}"])
    rate = (1 - w) * pre_rate + w * live_rate
    return np.maximum(rate.to_numpy() * v2_minutes(d, p) * d["pace_ratio"].to_numpy() ** p["pace_beta"], 1e-4)


V2_START = {"tau": 24.0, "foul": 0.3, "blow": 0.4, "bench": 0.95, "pace_beta": 0.5,
            "kappa_pts": 40.0, "kappa_reb": 40.0, "kappa_ast": 40.0, "kappa_fg3": 60.0}


def fit_v2(train: pd.DataFrame) -> dict:
    """Minimise total Poisson deviance of the remaining stat over all four stats (shared minutes model)."""
    keys = list(V2_START)

    def loss(x):
        p = dict(zip(keys, np.exp(x)))
        p["bench"] = min(p["bench"], 1.2)
        tot = 0.0
        for s in STATS:
            mu, y = v2_mean(train, s, p), train[f"{s}_rem"].to_numpy()
            tot += np.mean(mu - y * np.log(mu)) * (1.0 / max(y.mean(), 1e-3))  # scale-free per stat
        return tot

    x0 = np.log(list(V2_START.values()))
    res = optimize.minimize(loss, x0, method="Nelder-Mead", options={"maxiter": 3000, "xatol": 1e-3, "fatol": 1e-7})
    p = dict(zip(keys, np.exp(res.x)))
    p["bench"] = min(p["bench"], 1.2)
    return p


# ---------------------------------------------------------------- ml


ML_FEATS = ["frac", "elapsed", "min_sf", "on_court", "pf_sf", "margin", "pace_ratio", "pre_min", "pre_min_l5", "pre_n",
            "pre_pts", "pre_reb", "pre_ast", "pre_fg3", "pre_fga", "pre_fta", "pts_sf", "reb_sf", "ast_sf", "fg3_sf",
            "fga_sf", "fta_sf", "tov_sf"]


def fit_ml(train: pd.DataFrame, s: str):
    from sklearn.ensemble import HistGradientBoostingRegressor
    X = train[ML_FEATS].astype(float).to_numpy()
    m = HistGradientBoostingRegressor(loss="poisson", max_iter=400, learning_rate=0.05, min_samples_leaf=100,
                                      l2_regularization=1.0, random_state=0)
    m.fit(X, train[f"{s}_rem"].clip(lower=0).to_numpy())
    return m


def ml_mean(m, d: pd.DataFrame) -> np.ndarray:
    return np.maximum(m.predict(d[ML_FEATS].astype(float).to_numpy()), 1e-4)


# ---------------------------------------------------------------- distribution


def fit_dispersion(mu: np.ndarray, y: np.ndarray, bins: int = 12) -> tuple[np.ndarray, np.ndarray]:
    """Variance-to-mean ratio phi as a function of the predicted mean (binned on train). var = phi x mu."""
    edges = np.unique(np.quantile(mu, np.linspace(0, 1, bins + 1)))
    idx = np.clip(np.digitize(mu, edges[1:-1]), 0, len(edges) - 2)
    centers, phis = [], []
    for b in range(len(edges) - 1):
        m = idx == b
        if m.sum() < 50:
            continue
        centers.append(mu[m].mean())
        resid_var = np.mean((y[m] - mu[m]) ** 2)
        phis.append(max(resid_var / max(mu[m].mean(), 1e-6), 1.02))
    return np.asarray(centers), np.asarray(phis)


def p_at_least(mu: np.ndarray, need: np.ndarray, disp: tuple[np.ndarray, np.ndarray]) -> np.ndarray:
    """P(remaining >= need) under a negative binomial with var = phi(mu) x mu (need <= 0 -> 1)."""
    phi = np.interp(mu, disp[0], disp[1])
    r = mu / np.maximum(phi - 1, 1e-3)
    pnb = r / (r + mu)
    need = np.asarray(need, float)
    out = np.where(need <= 0, 1.0, sst.nbinom.sf(np.ceil(need) - 1, r, pnb))
    return np.clip(out, 1e-4, 1 - 1e-4)

=================== research/prop_pacer/build.py ===================
"""Build the in-game prop dataset from ESPN NBA play-by-play (sportsdataverse espn_nba_pbp parquet).

One row per player per game per break (end of Q1, Q2, Q3):
  so-far stats (pts, reb, ast, fg3, fga, fta, tov, pf), minutes played so far (rebuilt from substitutions),
  on court at the break, team / opponent possessions so far, score margin, break wallclock window,
  and the player's FINAL stats (overtime included, as Kalshi and sportsbooks settle).
Plus leakage-free pregame anchors: each player's exponentially weighted averages from his EARLIER games only.

usage: python research/prop_pacer/build.py OUT.parquet [nba|ncaab|ncaaw] PBP_season1.parquet PBP_season2.parquet ...
(NBA: espn_nba_pbp; men's college: espn_mens_college_basketball_pbp; women's: espn_womens_college_basketball_pbp)
"""

import sys

import numpy as np
import pandas as pd

STATS = ["pts", "reb", "ast", "fg3", "fga", "fta", "tov", "pf"]
# regulation periods, seconds per period, overtime seconds, fouls to foul out
LEAGUES = {"nba": (4, 720, 300, 6), "ncaab": (2, 1200, 300, 5), "ncaaw": (4, 600, 300, 5)}


def _events(g: pd.DataFrame) -> pd.DataFrame:
    """Per play: which player gets which stat (one row per player-stat credit)."""
    tt = g["type_text"].fillna("")
    a1, a2 = g["athlete_id_1"], g["athlete_id_2"]
    pa = g["points_attempted"].fillna(0)
    shoot = g["shooting_play"].fillna(False).astype(bool)
    made = g["scoring_play"].fillna(False).astype(bool)
    rows = []

    def add(mask, who, stat, val):
        m = mask & who.notna()
        if m.any():
            rows.append(pd.DataFrame({"i": g.index[m], "player": who[m].astype("int64"), "stat": stat,
                                      "val": np.asarray(val[m] if hasattr(val, "__getitem__") and not np.isscalar(val) else val)}))

    add(made & shoot, a1, "pts", g["score_value"].astype(float))
    add(made & shoot & (g["score_value"] == 3), a1, "fg3", pd.Series(1.0, index=g.index))
    add(made & shoot & pa.isin([2, 3]), a2, "ast", pd.Series(1.0, index=g.index))
    add(shoot & pa.isin([2, 3]), a1, "fga", pd.Series(1.0, index=g.index))
    add(shoot & (pa == 1), a1, "fta", pd.Series(1.0, index=g.index))
    add(tt.str.contains("Rebound") & ~g["text"].fillna("").str.contains("team rebound"), a1, "reb",
        pd.Series(1.0, index=g.index))
    add(tt.str.contains("Turnover"), a1, "tov", pd.Series(1.0, index=g.index))
    add(tt.str.contains("Foul") & ~tt.str.contains("Turnover|Technical"), a1, "pf", pd.Series(1.0, index=g.index))
    return pd.concat(rows, ignore_index=True) if rows else pd.DataFrame(columns=["i", "player", "stat", "val"])


def _minutes(g: pd.DataFrame, team_of: dict, league: str = "nba") -> pd.DataFrame:
    """Seconds on court per player per period from substitutions (players with an event in a period before entering
    it are on the floor at its start). Returns rows (player, period, sec_on, on_at_end)."""
    out = []
    # who comes in / goes out on each substitution: NBA "A enters the game for B" (A = id 1, B = id 2);
    # college has one event per player: "A subbing in" / "A subbing out"
    g = g.copy()
    txt = g["text"].fillna("")
    sub = g["type_text"] == "Substitution"
    college_in, college_out = sub & txt.str.contains("subbing in"), sub & txt.str.contains("subbing out")
    g["sub_in"] = np.where(college_in, g["athlete_id_1"], np.where(sub & ~college_out, g["athlete_id_1"], np.nan))
    g["sub_out"] = np.where(college_out, g["athlete_id_1"], np.where(sub & ~college_in, g["athlete_id_2"], np.nan))
    for per, pg in g.groupby("period_number", sort=True):
        n_reg, plen, ot, _ = LEAGUES[league]
        length = plen if per <= n_reg else ot
        start_rem = pg["clock_minutes"].astype(float) * 60 + pg["clock_seconds"].astype(float)  # seconds left in period
        first: dict[int, str] = {}
        for _, r in pg.iterrows():
            if r["type_text"] == "Substitution":
                for who, kind in ((r["sub_in"], "in"), (r["sub_out"], "out")):
                    if pd.notna(who) and int(who) not in first:
                        first[int(who)] = kind
            else:
                for who in (r["athlete_id_1"], r["athlete_id_2"], r["athlete_id_3"]):
                    if pd.notna(who) and int(who) not in first and int(who) in team_of:
                        first[int(who)] = "on"
        on = {p: length for p, k in first.items() if k in ("on", "out")}  # on court since t=length (seconds left)
        secs = {p: 0.0 for p in first}
        for (_, r), rem in zip(pg.iterrows(), start_rem):
            if r["type_text"] != "Substitution":
                continue
            rem = float(rem) if pd.notna(rem) else 0.0
            p_in, p_out = r["sub_in"], r["sub_out"]
            if pd.notna(p_out) and int(p_out) in on:
                secs[int(p_out)] += on.pop(int(p_out)) - rem
            if pd.notna(p_in) and int(p_in) not in on:
                on[int(p_in)] = rem
        for p, since in on.items():
            secs[p] = secs.get(p, 0.0) + since
        for p, s in secs.items():
            out.append((p, per, min(max(s, 0.0), length), p in on))
    return pd.DataFrame(out, columns=["player", "period", "sec", "on_end"])


def game_rows(g: pd.DataFrame, league: str = "nba") -> list[dict]:
    n_reg, plen, _, foul_out = LEAGUES[league]
    g = g.sort_values("game_play_number").reset_index(drop=True)
    for c in ("athlete_id_3", "athlete_name_2"):
        if c not in g:
            g[c] = np.nan
    if "points_attempted" not in g:  # older college files: shot value from the play text
        t = g["text"].fillna("")
        g["points_attempted"] = np.where(~g["shooting_play"].fillna(False).astype(bool), 0,
                                         np.where(t.str.contains("Free Throw"), 1,
                                                  np.where(t.str.contains("Three Point"), 3, 2)))
    if g["period_number"].max() < n_reg:
        return []
    home, away = g["home_team_id"].iloc[0], g["away_team_id"].iloc[0]
    ev = _events(g)
    if ev.empty:
        return []
    ev["period"] = g.loc[ev["i"], "period_number"].to_numpy()
    team_of = {}
    for _, r in g[g["athlete_id_1"].notna() & g["team_id"].notna() & (g["type_text"] != "Substitution")].iterrows():
        team_of.setdefault(int(r["athlete_id_1"]), r["team_id"])
    for _, r in g[(g["type_text"] == "Substitution") & g["team_id"].notna()].iterrows():
        for who in (r["athlete_id_1"], r["athlete_id_2"]):
            if pd.notna(who):
                team_of.setdefault(int(who), r["team_id"])
    mins = _minutes(g, team_of, league)
    names = {}
    for c_id, c_name in (("athlete_id_1", "athlete_name_1"), ("athlete_id_2", "athlete_name_2")):
        for pid, nm in zip(g[c_id], g[c_name]):
            if pd.notna(pid) and isinstance(nm, str):
                names.setdefault(int(pid), nm)
    # team possessions by period: FGA + 0.44 FTA - OREB + TOV
    tt = g["type_text"].fillna("")
    pa = g["points_attempted"].fillna(0)
    shoot = g["shooting_play"].fillna(False).astype(bool)
    poss = (shoot & pa.isin([2, 3])).astype(float) + 0.44 * (shoot & (pa == 1)).astype(float) \
        - (tt == "Offensive Rebound").astype(float) + tt.str.contains("Turnover").astype(float)
    tposs = pd.DataFrame({"team": g["team_id"], "period": g["period_number"], "poss": poss}).dropna()
    tposs = tposs.groupby(["team", "period"])["poss"].sum()
    ends = g.groupby("period_number").agg(last_wall=("wallclock", "last"), first_wall=("wallclock", "first"),
                                          hs=("home_score", "last"), as_=("away_score", "last"))
    players = sorted(set(ev["player"]) | set(mins["player"]))
    final = ev.pivot_table(index="player", columns="stat", values="val", aggfunc="sum").reindex(players).fillna(0)
    fmin = mins.groupby("player")["sec"].sum().reindex(players).fillna(0) / 60
    out = []
    meta = {"game_id": int(g["game_id"].iloc[0]), "date": str(g["game_date"].iloc[0]),
            "season": int(g["season"].iloc[0]), "season_type": int(g["season_type"].iloc[0]),
            "home": g["home_team_name"].iloc[0], "away": g["away_team_name"].iloc[0],
            "home_abbr": g["home_team_abbrev"].iloc[0], "away_abbr": g["away_team_abbrev"].iloc[0],
            "periods": int(g["period_number"].max()), "league": league, "game_min": n_reg * plen / 60,
            "foul_out": foul_out,
            "final_home": float(ends["hs"].iloc[-1]), "final_away": float(ends["as_"].iloc[-1])}
    for k in range(1, n_reg):
        if k + 1 not in ends.index:
            continue
        so = ev[ev["period"] <= k].pivot_table(index="player", columns="stat", values="val", aggfunc="sum")
        so = so.reindex(players).fillna(0)
        mk = mins[mins["period"] <= k].groupby("player")["sec"].sum().reindex(players).fillna(0) / 60
        on_end = mins[mins["period"] == k].set_index("player")["on_end"].reindex(players).fillna(False)
        hs, as_ = float(ends.loc[k, "hs"]), float(ends.loc[k, "as_"])
        for p in players:
            t = team_of.get(p)
            if t is None or fmin.get(p, 0) <= 0 and final.loc[p].sum() == 0:
                continue
            is_home = t == home
            opp = away if is_home else home
            row = {**meta, "player": int(p), "name": names.get(p, ""), "team_id": t, "is_home": is_home, "k": k,
                   "elapsed": plen / 60 * k, "frac": k / n_reg, "min_sf": float(mk[p]), "on_court": bool(on_end[p]),
                   "min_final": float(fmin[p]),
                   "tposs_sf": float(sum(tposs.get((t, q), 0) for q in range(1, k + 1))),
                   "oposs_sf": float(sum(tposs.get((opp, q), 0) for q in range(1, k + 1))),
                   "margin": (hs - as_) * (1 if is_home else -1),
                   "break_start": ends.loc[k, "last_wall"], "break_end": ends.loc[k + 1, "first_wall"]}
            for s in STATS:
                row[f"{s}_sf"] = float(so.loc[p].get(s, 0.0))
                row[f"{s}_final"] = float(final.loc[p].get(s, 0.0))
            out.append(row)
    return out


def anchors(df: pd.DataFrame) -> pd.DataFrame:
    """Leakage-free pregame expectations from each player's earlier games (EWM, half-life 10 games)."""
    games = df[df["k"] == 1][["player", "game_id", "date", "team_id", "min_final",
                              *[f"{s}_final" for s in STATS]]].drop_duplicates(["player", "game_id"])
    games = games.sort_values(["player", "date", "game_id"])
    g = games.groupby("player", sort=False)
    for c in ["min_final", "pts_final", "reb_final", "ast_final", "fg3_final", "fga_final", "fta_final", "pf_final"]:
        games[f"pre_{c[:-6]}"] = g[c].transform(lambda s: s.shift().ewm(halflife=10, min_periods=1).mean())
    games["pre_n"] = g.cumcount()
    games["pre_min_l5"] = g["min_final"].transform(lambda s: s.shift().rolling(5, min_periods=1).mean())
    # team pace prior: possessions per 48 from earlier games
    last = df.groupby("game_id")["k"].transform("max")
    tg = df[df["k"] == last].groupby(["game_id", "team_id", "date"], as_index=False)[["tposs_sf", "elapsed"]].first()
    tg["pace36"] = tg["tposs_sf"] / tg["elapsed"] * 36  # possessions per 36 minutes
    tg = tg.sort_values(["team_id", "date"])
    tg["pre_pace36"] = tg.groupby("team_id")["pace36"].transform(lambda s: s.shift().ewm(halflife=10, min_periods=1).mean())
    keep = ["player", "game_id"] + [c for c in games.columns if c.startswith("pre_")]
    df = df.merge(games[keep], on=["player", "game_id"], how="left")
    df = df.merge(tg[["game_id", "team_id", "pre_pace36"]], on=["game_id", "team_id"], how="left")
    return df


def main(out: str, *paths: str) -> None:
    league = "nba"
    if paths and paths[0] in LEAGUES:
        league, paths = paths[0], paths[1:]
    rows = []
    for path in paths:
        import pyarrow.parquet as pq
        have = set(pq.read_schema(path).names)
        p = pd.read_parquet(path, columns=[c for c in ["game_id", "game_play_number", "type_text", "text", "athlete_id_1",
                                           "athlete_id_2", "athlete_id_3", "athlete_name_1", "athlete_name_2",
                                           "team_id", "scoring_play", "score_value", "shooting_play",
                                           "points_attempted", "period_number", "clock_minutes", "clock_seconds",
                                           "home_score", "away_score", "wallclock", "game_date", "season",
                                           "season_type", "home_team_id", "away_team_id", "home_team_name",
                                           "away_team_name", "home_team_abbrev", "away_team_abbrev"] if c in have])
        p = p[p["season_type"].isin([2, 3])]
        for gid, g in p.groupby("game_id", sort=False):
            try:
                rows += game_rows(g, league)
            except Exception as exc:  # a malformed game never stops the build
                print("skip", gid, exc, file=sys.stderr)
        print(path, len(rows), file=sys.stderr)
    df = anchors(pd.DataFrame(rows))
    df.to_parquet(out)
    print(out, df.shape)

=================== research/prop_pacer/evaluate.py ===================
"""Walk-forward test of the in-game prop models. Train = earlier seasons, test = the latest season (never seen).

  1. remaining-stat accuracy (MAE, Poisson deviance) per model, stat, break
  2. probability quality at Kalshi-style ladders (Brier, calibration table)
  3. real-market test vs Kalshi prices at the breaks (if the price file exists):
       Brier of each model vs the market's own mid price on the same contracts,
       and paper bets (YES at the ask / NO at 1 - bid, Kalshi taker fee 0.07 p (1-p)) when edge >= threshold.
       Thresholds / the model-market blend are chosen on the first 40% of the price sample (by date) and reported
       on the last 60% only.

usage: python research/prop_pacer/evaluate.py SNAP.parquet [KALSHI.jsonl] OUT_DIR
"""

import json
import os
import sys
import unicodedata
from pathlib import Path

import numpy as np
import pandas as pd

sys.path.insert(0, str(Path(__file__).resolve().parent))
import models as M  # noqa: E402

LADDER = {"pts": [10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40], "reb": [4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14], "ast": [2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12],
          "fg3": [1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6]}
MODELS = ["naive", "prop1", "v2", "ml"]


def norm(name: str) -> str:
    n = unicodedata.normalize("NFKD", str(name)).encode("ascii", "ignore").decode().lower()
    for x in (".", "'", "-", ",", " jr", " sr", " iii", " ii", " iv"):
        n = n.replace(x, " " if x == "-" else "")
    return " ".join(n.split())


def fee(p):
    return 0.07 * p * (1 - p)


def means(d: pd.DataFrame, fitted: dict) -> dict:
    out = {}
    for s in M.STATS:
        out[("naive", s)] = np.maximum(M.naive(d, s), 1e-4)
        out[("prop1", s)] = np.maximum(M.prop1(d, s), 1e-4)
        out[("v2", s)] = M.v2_mean(d, s, fitted["v2"])
        out[("ml", s)] = M.ml_mean(fitted["ml"][s], d)
    return out


def logit(p):
    p = np.clip(p, 1e-4, 1 - 1e-4)
    return np.log(p / (1 - p))


def main(snap: str, *rest: str) -> None:
    out_dir = Path(rest[-1])
    kalshi = rest[0] if len(rest) == 2 else None
    out_dir.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)
    df = M.usable(pd.read_parquet(snap))
    test_season = df["season"].max()
    train, test = df[df["season"] < test_season], df[df["season"] == test_season]
    print(f"train {len(train):,} rows (seasons {sorted(train.season.unique())}), test {len(test):,} rows ({test_season})")
    fitted = {"v2": M.fit_v2(train.sample(min(len(train), 120000), random_state=0))}
    print("v2 parameters:", {k: round(v, 3) for k, v in fitted["v2"].items()})
    fitted["ml"] = {s: M.fit_ml(train, s) for s in M.STATS}
    mtr, mte = means(train, fitted), means(test, fitted)
    disp = {key: M.fit_dispersion(mtr[key], train[f"{key[1]}_rem"].to_numpy()) for key in mtr}

    report = {"league": str(df["league"].iloc[0]), "train_rows": len(train), "test_rows": len(test), "test_season": int(test_season),
              "v2_params": fitted["v2"], "accuracy": [], "brier": [], "calibration": {}, "market": {}}
    # 1. accuracy
    for s in M.STATS:
        y = test[f"{s}_rem"].to_numpy()
        for k in sorted(test["k"].unique()):
            m = (test["k"] == k).to_numpy()
            row = {"stat": s, "k": int(k), "n": int(m.sum())}
            for name in MODELS:
                mu = mte[(name, s)][m]
                row[f"{name}_mae"] = float(np.mean(np.abs(mu - y[m])))
                row[f"{name}_dev"] = float(np.mean(2 * (np.where(y[m] > 0, y[m] * np.log(np.maximum(y[m], 1e-9) / mu), 0) - (y[m] - mu))))
            report["accuracy"].append(row)
    # 2. ladder Brier + calibration
    for s in M.STATS:
        for name in MODELS:
            ps, ys = [], []
            for T in LADDER[s]:
                need = T - test[f"{s}_sf"].to_numpy()
                keep = need > 0
                p = M.p_at_least(mte[(name, s)][keep], need[keep], disp[(name, s)])
                ps.append(p)
                ys.append((test[f"{s}_final"].to_numpy()[keep] >= T).astype(float))
            p, y = np.concatenate(ps), np.concatenate(ys)
            report["brier"].append({"stat": s, "model": name, "n": int(len(p)), "brier": float(np.mean((p - y) ** 2)),
                                    "logloss": float(-np.mean(y * np.log(p) + (1 - y) * np.log(1 - p)))})
            bins = np.linspace(0, 1, 11)
            idx = np.clip(np.digitize(p, bins) - 1, 0, 9)
            report["calibration"][f"{name}:{s}"] = [
                {"bin": f"{bins[b]:.1f}-{bins[b + 1]:.1f}", "n": int((idx == b).sum()),
                 "pred": float(p[idx == b].mean()) if (idx == b).any() else None,
                 "actual": float(y[idx == b].mean()) if (idx == b).any() else None} for b in range(10)]
    # 3. Kalshi
    if kalshi and Path(kalshi).exists():
        report["market"] = market_test(test, mte, disp, kalshi)
    (out_dir / "prop_pacer_backtest.json").write_text(json.dumps(report, indent=1, default=float))
    write_md(report, out_dir / "prop_pacer_backtest.md")
    print((out_dir / "prop_pacer_backtest.md").read_text())


def market_test(test: pd.DataFrame, mte: dict, disp: dict, kalshi: str) -> dict:
    k = pd.DataFrame([json.loads(l) for l in open(kalshi)])
    k = k[k["yes_bid"].notna() & k["yes_ask"].notna() & k["result"].isin(["yes", "no"])]
    k["key"] = k["player_name"].map(norm)
    t = test.reset_index(drop=True).copy()
    t["key"] = t["name"].map(norm)
    t["_row"] = np.arange(len(t))
    j = k.merge(t[["game_id", "k", "key", "_row", "date", "pts_sf", "reb_sf", "ast_sf", "fg3_sf", "pts_final",
                   "reb_final", "ast_final", "fg3_final"]], on=["game_id", "k", "key"], how="inner")
    if j.empty:
        return {"matched": 0}
    j["so_far"] = [r[f"{s}_sf"] for r, s in zip(j.to_dict("records"), j["stat"])]
    j["final"] = [r[f"{s}_final"] for r, s in zip(j.to_dict("records"), j["stat"])]
    j["need"] = j["strike"] + 0.5 - j["so_far"]
    j["y"] = (j["result"] == "yes").astype(float)
    # sanity: settlement agrees with the play-by-play box score
    agree = float(((j["final"] > j["strike"]).astype(float) == j["y"]).mean())
    j = j[j["need"] > 0]  # already-cashed rungs are not bets
    j["mid"] = (j["yes_bid"] + j["yes_ask"]) / 2
    for name in MODELS:
        p = np.empty(len(j))
        for s in M.STATS:
            m = (j["stat"] == s).to_numpy()
            if m.any():
                rows = j.loc[m, "_row"].to_numpy()
                p[m] = M.p_at_least(mte[(name, s)][rows], j.loc[m, "need"].to_numpy(), disp[(name, s)])
        j[f"p_{name}"] = p
    j = j.sort_values("date")
    if os.environ.get("PACER_SAVE_MARKET"):
        j.to_parquet(os.environ["PACER_SAVE_MARKET"])  # per-contract table for bootstrap / slicing
    cut = int(len(j) * 0.4)
    cal, ev = j.iloc[:cut].copy(), j.iloc[cut:].copy()
    res = {"matched": int(len(j)), "settle_agree": agree, "games": int(j["game_id"].nunique()),
           "calib_rows": int(len(cal)), "eval_rows": int(len(ev)),
           "eval_dates": [str(ev["date"].min()), str(ev["date"].max())], "brier": {}, "bets": {}}
    res["brier"]["market_mid"] = float(np.mean((ev["mid"] - ev["y"]) ** 2))
    for name in MODELS:
        res["brier"][name] = float(np.mean((ev[f"p_{name}"] - ev["y"]) ** 2))
        # blend with the market: logistic fit on the calibration part only
        from sklearn.linear_model import LogisticRegression
        X = np.c_[logit(cal[f"p_{name}"]), logit(cal["mid"])]
        lr = LogisticRegression(C=10).fit(X, cal["y"])
        ev[f"p_{name}_blend"] = lr.predict_proba(np.c_[logit(ev[f"p_{name}"]), logit(ev["mid"])])[:, 1]
        cal[f"p_{name}_blend"] = lr.predict_proba(X)[:, 1]  # in-sample on the calibration part (optimistic by design)
        res["brier"][f"{name}_blend"] = float(np.mean((ev[f"p_{name}_blend"] - ev["y"]) ** 2))
        res["brier"][f"{name}_blend_weights"] = [float(x) for x in lr.coef_[0]]
    for col in [f"p_{n}" for n in MODELS] + [f"p_{n}_blend" for n in MODELS]:
        res["bets"][col] = {}
        for thr in (0.02, 0.04, 0.06, 0.08, 0.10, 0.15):
            res["bets"][col][str(thr)] = bet(ev, col, thr)
            res["bets"][col][f"calib_{thr}"] = bet(cal, col, thr)
    # Stale-quote check: only quotes Kalshi refreshed after the period ended (a stale quote fakes an edge)
    if "since_period_end_s" in ev:
        fresh = ev[ev["since_period_end_s"] >= 0]
        res["fresh_rows"] = int(len(fresh))
        res["brier_fresh"] = {"market_mid": float(np.mean((fresh["mid"] - fresh["y"]) ** 2))} if len(fresh) else {}
        res["bets_fresh"] = {}
        for name in MODELS:
            if len(fresh):
                res["brier_fresh"][name] = float(np.mean((fresh[f"p_{name}"] - fresh["y"]) ** 2))
            res["bets_fresh"][f"p_{name}"] = {str(t): bet(fresh, f"p_{name}", t) for t in (0.04, 0.06, 0.08, 0.10)}
    return res


def bet(d: pd.DataFrame, col: str, thr: float) -> dict:
    p = d[col].to_numpy()
    ya, yb, y = d["yes_ask"].to_numpy(), d["yes_bid"].to_numpy(), d["y"].to_numpy()
    na = 1 - yb
    ok = (ya - yb <= 0.10) & (yb >= 0.02) & (ya <= 0.98)
    e_yes = p - ya - fee(ya)
    e_no = (1 - p) - na - fee(na)
    buy_yes = ok & (e_yes >= thr) & (e_yes >= e_no) & (ya >= 0.05)
    buy_no = ok & (e_no >= thr) & (e_no > e_yes) & (na >= 0.05)
    cost = np.where(buy_yes, ya + fee(ya), np.where(buy_no, na + fee(na), 0))
    win = np.where(buy_yes, y, np.where(buy_no, 1 - y, 0))
    n = int((buy_yes | buy_no).sum())
    staked = float(cost.sum())
    pnl = float((win * (buy_yes | buy_no)).sum() - staked)
    return {"n": n, "yes": int(buy_yes.sum()), "no": int(buy_no.sum()),
            "win_rate": float(win[buy_yes | buy_no].mean()) if n else None,
            "staked": round(staked, 2), "pnl": round(pnl, 2), "roi": pnl / staked if staked else None}


LABEL = {"naive": "naive pacer", "prop1": "Claude Prop 1", "v2": "minutes model", "ml": "ML"}


def _label(col: str) -> str:
    base = col[2:] if col.startswith("p_") else col
    blend = base.endswith("_blend")
    base = base.replace("_blend", "")
    return LABEL.get(base, base) + (" + market blend" if blend else "")


def write_md(r: dict, path: Path) -> None:
    L = [f"# In-game {r.get('league', 'nba').upper()} prop pacer backtest (test season {r['test_season']}, never seen in training)",
         f"train rows {r['train_rows']:,} · test rows {r['test_rows']:,}", "",
         "## 1. Remaining-stat accuracy (MAE, lower is better)", "",
         "| stat | break | n | naive pacer | Claude Prop 1 | minutes model | ML |", "|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|"]
    for a in r["accuracy"]:
        L.append(f"| {a['stat']} | {'half' if r.get('league') == 'ncaab' else 'Q' + str(a['k'])} | {a['n']:,} | {a['naive_mae']:.2f} | {a['prop1_mae']:.2f} | "
                 f"{a['v2_mae']:.2f} | {a['ml_mae']:.2f} |")
    L += ["", "## 2. Ladder probabilities (Brier, lower is better)", "", "| stat | naive pacer | Claude Prop 1 | minutes model | ML |",
          "|---|---|---|---|---|"]
    for s in M.STATS:
        b = {x["model"]: x["brier"] for x in r["brier"] if x["stat"] == s}
        L.append(f"| {s} | " + " | ".join(f"{b[m]:.4f}" for m in MODELS) + " |")
    m = r.get("market") or {}
    if m.get("matched"):
        L += ["", f"## 3. Real Kalshi prices at the breaks ({m['games']} games, {m['matched']:,} contracts; "
                  f"evaluation part {m['eval_rows']:,} rows, {m['eval_dates'][0]} to {m['eval_dates'][1]})",
              f"Kalshi settlement agrees with the play-by-play box score on {m['settle_agree']:.1%} of contracts.", "",
              "Brier on the evaluation part (lower is better; the market's own mid price is the bar):", ""]
        for k2, v in m["brier"].items():
            if not k2.endswith("weights"):
                L.append(f"- {_label('p_' + k2) if k2 != 'market_mid' else 'Kalshi mid price'}: {v:.4f}")
        L += ["", "Paper bets on the evaluation part (YES at ask / NO at 1 - bid, after Kalshi fee):", "",
              "| model | edge >= | bets | win rate | staked $ | P&L $ | ROI | (calibration part ROI) |",
              "|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|"]
        for col, by in m["bets"].items():
            for thr in ("0.04", "0.06", "0.08", "0.1"):
                b, c = by.get(thr), by.get(f"calib_{thr}")
                if not b or not b["n"]:
                    continue
                croi = f"{c['roi']:+.1%}" if c and c["roi"] is not None else "-"
                L.append(f"| {_label(col)} | {thr} | {b['n']} | {b['win_rate']:.1%} | {b['staked']:.0f} | {b['pnl']:+.1f} | "
                         f"{b['roi']:+.1%} | {croi} |")
    if m.get("fresh_rows"):
        L += ["", f"Only quotes refreshed after the period ended ({m['fresh_rows']:,} rows of the evaluation part):", ""]
        L += [f"- Brier {('Kalshi mid price' if k2 == 'market_mid' else LABEL[k2])}: {v:.4f}" for k2, v in m["brier_fresh"].items()]
        L += ["", "| model | edge >= | bets | win rate | P&L $ | ROI |", "|---|---|---|---|---|---|"]
        for col, by in m["bets_fresh"].items():
            for thr, b in by.items():
                if b["n"]:
                    L.append(f"| {_label(col)} | {thr} | {b['n']} | {b['win_rate']:.1%} | {b['pnl']:+.1f} | {b['roi']:+.1%} |")
    path.write_text("\n".join(L) + "\n")


if __name__ == "__main__":
    main(*sys.argv[1:])

=================== research/prop_pacer/fetch_kalshi.py ===================
"""Real in-game Kalshi prices for NBA player props at each quarter break (public API, no account).

For a seeded random sample of games in the snapshot file: list the game's settled KXNBAPTS / KXNBAREB / KXNBAAST /
KXNBA3PT markets (historical endpoint), then pull each market's 1-minute candlesticks over the game and keep, for
every break (end of Q1, Q2, Q3), the LAST candle before play resumes: yes bid / yes ask close, volume during the
break. The quote kept is the one standing when play resumes (last candle at or before the break's end) with its age, so
quotes that were not refreshed after the period ended can be filtered out in the evaluation.

usage: python research/prop_pacer/fetch_kalshi.py SNAP.parquet OUT.jsonl [n_games] [seed]
Resumable: games already in OUT are skipped.
"""

import json
import random
import sys
import time
from datetime import datetime
from pathlib import Path

import pandas as pd
import requests

API = "https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2"
SERIES = {"KXNBAPTS": "pts", "KXNBAREB": "reb", "KXNBAAST": "ast", "KXNBA3PT": "fg3"}
ABBR = {"GS": "GSW", "NO": "NOP", "NY": "NYK", "SA": "SAS", "UTAH": "UTA", "WSH": "WAS"}
MON = ["JAN", "FEB", "MAR", "APR", "MAY", "JUN", "JUL", "AUG", "SEP", "OCT", "NOV", "DEC"]
session = requests.Session()
_last = [0.0]


def get(path: str, params: dict, tries: int = 6) -> dict:
    for i in range(tries):
        wait = 0.3 - (time.time() - _last[0])  # ~3 requests a second: the historical endpoints rate-limit above that
        if wait > 0:
            time.sleep(wait)
        _last[0] = time.time()
        try:
            r = session.get(API + path, params=params, timeout=20)
        except requests.RequestException:
            time.sleep(2 ** i)
            continue
        if r.status_code == 429:
            time.sleep(1.5)
            continue
        if r.status_code == 404:
            return {}
        r.raise_for_status()
        return r.json()
    return {}


def ts(s: str) -> int:
    return int(datetime.fromisoformat(str(s).replace("Z", "+00:00")).timestamp())


def event_suffix(row) -> list[str]:
    d = datetime.fromisoformat(str(row["date"])[:10])
    day = f"{d:%y}{MON[d.month - 1]}{d:%d}"
    a, h = ABBR.get(row["away_abbr"], row["away_abbr"]), ABBR.get(row["home_abbr"], row["home_abbr"])
    return [f"{day}{a}{h}", f"{day}{h}{a}"]


def _norm(n: str) -> str:
    return " ".join(str(n).lower().replace(".", "").replace("'", "").replace("-", " ").split())


def game_prices(g: pd.DataFrame, q1: dict | None = None) -> list[dict]:
    """q1: {(normalised name, stat): value at the end of Q1}; rungs already reached by then are never bets, skipped."""
    row = g.iloc[0]
    breaks = g.groupby("k")[["break_start", "break_end"]].first()
    out = []
    for suffix in event_suffix(row):
        markets = []
        for series in SERIES:
            markets += [(series, m) for m in get("/historical/markets", {"event_ticker": f"{series}-{suffix}",
                                                                          "limit": 1000}).get("markets", [])]
        if markets:
            break
    if not markets:
        return []
    t0 = ts(breaks["break_start"].min()) - 120
    t1 = ts(breaks["break_end"].max()) + 120
    for series, m in markets:
        have = (q1 or {}).get((_norm(m["title"].split(":")[0]), SERIES[series]))
        if have is not None and m.get("floor_strike") is not None and have > m["floor_strike"]:
            continue
        c = get(f"/historical/markets/{m['ticker']}/candlesticks",
                {"start_ts": t0, "end_ts": t1, "period_interval": 1}).get("candlesticks", [])
        if not c:
            continue
        for k, b in breaks.iterrows():
            bs, be = ts(b["break_start"]), ts(b["break_end"])
            # Kalshi emits a candle only when something changes, so the quote standing when play resumes is the last
            # candle at or before the end of the break. Its age is kept so stale quotes can be filtered.
            before = [x for x in c if x["end_period_ts"] <= be]
            if not before:
                continue
            last = before[-1]
            inside = [x for x in before if x["end_period_ts"] >= bs]
            yb, ya = (last.get("yes_bid") or {}).get("close"), (last.get("yes_ask") or {}).get("close")
            vol = sum(float(x.get("volume") or x.get("volume_fp") or 0) for x in inside)
            out.append({"game_id": int(row["game_id"]), "k": int(k), "series": series, "stat": SERIES[series],
                        "ticker": m["ticker"], "title": m["title"], "player_name": m["title"].split(":")[0].strip(),
                        "strike": m.get("floor_strike"), "result": m.get("result"),
                        "yes_bid": None if yb is None else float(yb), "yes_ask": None if ya is None else float(ya),
                        "break_volume": vol, "price_ts": last["end_period_ts"], "age_s": be - last["end_period_ts"],
                        "since_period_end_s": last["end_period_ts"] - bs})
    return out


def main(snap: str, out: str, n: int = 300, seed: int = 7) -> None:
    df = pd.read_parquet(snap, columns=["game_id", "date", "season", "k", "home_abbr", "away_abbr", "break_start",
                                        "break_end", "name", "pts_sf", "reb_sf", "ast_sf", "fg3_sf"])
    df = df[df["season"] == df["season"].max()]
    games = sorted(df["game_id"].unique())
    random.Random(seed).shuffle(games)
    done = set()
    if Path(out).exists():
        done |= {json.loads(line)["game_id"] for line in open(out)}
    if Path(out + ".empty").exists():
        done |= {int(line.split()[1]) for line in open(out + ".empty") if line.startswith("empty")}
    todo = [g for g in games[:n] if g not in done]
    print(f"{len(todo)} games to fetch", flush=True)
    by = df.drop_duplicates(["game_id", "k"]).groupby("game_id")
    q1all = df[df["k"] == 1].groupby("game_id")
    for i, gid in enumerate(todo):
        q = q1all.get_group(gid)
        q1 = {(_norm(n), s): v for n, *vals in q[["name", "pts_sf", "reb_sf", "ast_sf", "fg3_sf"]].itertuples(index=False)
              for s, v in zip(("pts", "reb", "ast", "fg3"), vals)}
        rows = game_prices(by.get_group(gid), q1)
        with open(out, "a") as fh:
            for r in rows:
                fh.write(json.dumps(r) + "\n")
        if not rows:
            with open(out + ".empty", "a") as fh:
                fh.write(f"empty {gid}\n")
        print(f"{i + 1}/{len(todo)} game {gid}: {len(rows)} prices", flush=True)


if __name__ == "__main__":
    main(sys.argv[1], sys.argv[2], *(int(x) for x in sys.argv[3:]))

=================== research/prop_pacer/bootstrap.py ===================
"""Uncertainty of the Kalshi paper-bet ROI: resample whole GAMES (bets in one game are not independent).

usage: python research/prop_pacer/bootstrap.py MARKET_ROWS.parquet
MARKET_ROWS is the per-contract table evaluate.py writes when PACER_SAVE_MARKET=path is set.
"""
import sys

import numpy as np
import pandas as pd

sys.path.insert(0, __import__("os").path.dirname(__file__))
from evaluate import fee  # noqa: E402


def bets(d: pd.DataFrame, col: str, thr: float) -> pd.DataFrame:
    p, ya, yb, y = d[col].to_numpy(), d["yes_ask"].to_numpy(), d["yes_bid"].to_numpy(), d["y"].to_numpy()
    na = 1 - yb
    ok = (ya - yb <= 0.10) & (yb >= 0.02) & (ya <= 0.98)
    e_yes, e_no = p - ya - fee(ya), (1 - p) - na - fee(na)
    by = ok & (e_yes >= thr) & (e_yes >= e_no) & (ya >= 0.05)
    bn = ok & (e_no >= thr) & (e_no > e_yes) & (na >= 0.05)
    cost = np.where(by, ya + fee(ya), na + fee(na))
    win = np.where(by, y, 1 - y)
    out = d[by | bn].copy()
    out["cost"], out["pnl"], out["side"] = cost[by | bn], (win - cost)[by | bn], np.where(by, "yes", "no")[by | bn]
    return out


def main(path: str) -> None:
    d = pd.read_parquet(path)
    rng = np.random.default_rng(0)
    games = d["game_id"].unique()
    print(f"{len(d):,} contracts, {len(games)} games, {d['date'].min()} to {d['date'].max()} (whole sample)")
    for col in ("p_naive", "p_prop1", "p_v2", "p_ml"):
        for thr in (0.04, 0.06, 0.08):
            b = bets(d, col, thr)
            if b.empty:
                continue
            g = b.groupby("game_id")[["pnl", "cost"]].sum().reindex(games, fill_value=0)
            rois = []
            for _ in range(2000):
                s = g.iloc[rng.integers(0, len(g), len(g))].sum()
                rois.append(s["pnl"] / s["cost"] if s["cost"] else 0)
            lo, hi = np.percentile(rois, [5, 95])
            roi = b["pnl"].sum() / b["cost"].sum()
            print(f"{col[2:]:6s} edge>={thr:.2f}: {len(b):4d} bets ({(b.side == 'yes').mean():.0%} YES) ROI {roi:+.1%} "
                  f"90% range {lo:+.1%} .. {hi:+.1%}  P(ROI>0) {np.mean(np.array(rois) > 0):.0%}")
    b = bets(d, "p_v2", 0.06)
    print("\nminutes model, edge >= 6c, by slice (whole sample):")
    for key in ("k", "stat", "side"):
        t = b.groupby(key).agg(n=("pnl", "size"), pnl=("pnl", "sum"), cost=("cost", "sum"))
        t["roi"] = t["pnl"] / t["cost"]
        print(t.round(3).to_string(), "\n")


if __name__ == "__main__":
    main(sys.argv[1])
